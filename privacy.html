Privacy Policy

media agent is used only by its operator, Simon-Olivier Lirette, to send his own posts to his own TikTok account.

Data accessed: the TikTok access and refresh tokens granted at sign-in, and basic profile information (user.info.basic). The tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to upload the operator's posts to the TikTok inbox. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared.

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security, Apps and services.
